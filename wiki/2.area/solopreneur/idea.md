# IDEA Phase

> If you can't explain who pays and why in 2 sentences, the idea isn't ready.

## Overview

48 hours max. Validate before writing a single line of code. Most ideas die here — that's the point.

## Process

```
Idea ──▶ Problem Check ──▶ Audience Check ──▶ Revenue Check ──▶ GO / NO-GO
  │            │                 │                 │
  │         "Who has           "Can I            "Will they
  │          this problem?"    reach them?"       pay $X?"
  │            │                 │                 │
  ▼            ▼                 ▼                 ▼
Dump      Skip if vague     Skip if no        Skip if free
ideas     or "everyone"     clear channel     alternative exists
```

## Checklist

Answer every question before moving to BUILD:

### Problem

- [ ] What specific problem does this solve? (one sentence)
- [ ] Who has this problem RIGHT NOW? (not "everyone")
- [ ] How are they solving it today? (your competition)
- [ ] Why is the current solution painful/annoying/expensive?

### Market

- [ ] Where does this audience hang out? (X, Reddit, Discord, IH)
- [ ] Can I reach 100 potential users in a week?
- [ ] Are people already searching for this? (Google Trends, Gumroad, Product Hunt)

### Revenue

- [ ] What will I charge? (pick a number, not "TBD")
- [ ] Is this a one-time purchase or subscription?
- [ ] Can I find 10 people who'd pre-order or join a waitlist?
- [ ] What's the MVP revenue target? (aim: $100 MRR in 30 days)

### Kill Signals

| Signal                              | Action                                       |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| "Everyone could use this"           | Kill. No one specifically needs it           |
| No clear pricing model              | Kill. You don't know who pays                |
| Building for yourself only          | Proceed with caution. Validate with others   |
| Can't find where audience hangs out | Kill. No distribution channel = dead product |
| Free alternative does 80% of it     | Kill unless you can clearly differentiate    |

## Tips

- **Stolen ideas are fine** — see a product making $5k MRR? Build a better/faster/cheaper version
- **Scratch your own itch** — best if you're the target user AND can reach similar people
- **B2B > B2C** — businesses pay easier than consumers. Charge more, churn less
- **Micro-SaaS** — small market, high willingness to pay, no VC needed
