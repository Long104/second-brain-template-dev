## How to Use

1. **Daily note** — capture what you're feeling blocked on in `## Morning Check-in → Blockers`
2. **Here** — if it's a recurring block, add it to Active Blockers with a root cause + fix plan
3. **Fix plan** — write concrete actions, not vague ideas
4. **Solved** — when fixed, move to Solved Blockers with what actually worked
