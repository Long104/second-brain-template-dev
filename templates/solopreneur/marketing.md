# [Product Name] — Marketing

> Launch assets, content calendar, distribution posts. Human voice only.

## Voice Rules

- Short sentences. Numbers > adjectives. No emoji spam.
- Honest about struggles and failures.
- Never: "Excited to announce", "game-changing", "revolutionary"
- Always: real numbers, specific details, real screenshots
